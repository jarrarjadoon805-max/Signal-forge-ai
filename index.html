METHODOLOGY

How SignalForge AI Works

We designed SignalForge around strict factual grounding to give creators and marketers trustworthy material.

1 Automated Evidence Gathering

When you enter a topic or marketing problem, the engine decomposes it into discrete research sub-queries. It identifies authoritative domains, peer-reviewed sources, verified reporting, and real market statistics.

2 Contradiction & Claim Verification

Before drafting a single line of script or recommendation, the engine looks for conflicting evidence. If an online claim is outdated or unverifiable, SignalForge flags it in the audit report rather than presenting it as fact.

3 Targeted Deliverable Engineering

For creators, the synthesized facts are organized into narrative beats with pacing directions and visual cues. For marketers, the data is translated into customer pain points, moat comparisons, and actionable go-to-market experiments.
